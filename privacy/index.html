Music Memo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Music Memo ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the game experience:

  • Account data — your email address, display name, and avatar. This is created when you sign in with email/password, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple, and is stored with our backend provider (Supabase).
  • Game data — your game history, scores, statistics, daily challenge scores, free-tier play counts, and subscription status. This lets us show your statistics, leaderboards, and enforce daily free-game limits.
  • Purchase data — subscription status and entitlements processed through RevenueCat and the Apple App Store. We never see or store your full payment details; Apple handles those.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To save and display your game statistics and history
  • To manage free-tier limits and subscription access
  • To provide online multiplayer features (matchmaking, invite codes, game sessions)
  • To support you if you contact us

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting. supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat — in-app subscription management. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple / Google — used only for sign-in (Apple ID / Google account).
  • Pixabay — some in-game sound effects are licensed from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License. pixabay.com/service/license-summary

4. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use analytics SDKs, do not serve ads, and do not sell or share your personal information with anyone for marketing purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Music Memo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data, and you may withdraw consent or close your account at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, through the app.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: support@musicmemo.app
